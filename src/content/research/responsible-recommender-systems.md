Across these topics, I examine how information access, measurement, assumptions, and evaluation choices affect the conclusions a model supports. I aim to make these choices inspectable, retain material uncertainty, and consider differences across users and contexts. This connects simulation validation with broader questions of bias, fairness, and responsible AI.
