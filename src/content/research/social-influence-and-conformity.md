People differ in their priorities, experiences, and responses to uncertainty. They also change their behaviour as needs evolve, information becomes available, and social influences develop. I study these sources of variation through explicit behavioural models and simulation.

This work connects needs evolution, population-level change, conformity, and personality-related modelling. It examines how these factors can affect individual choices and the behaviour of recommender systems, including under disruptive events. Observed behavioural signatures help construct model-based representations; their psychological interpretation requires evidence suited to the construct being represented.
