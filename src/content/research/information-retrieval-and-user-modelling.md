AI agents increasingly search, compare information, and carry out tasks on behalf of people. These interactions raise questions about how human goals and constraints are translated into agent actions, and whose preferences are reflected in the resulting behavioural records.

I am interested in how delegation changes decision processes and feedback between people and information systems. Evaluating successful delegation requires evidence about the person’s goals and constraints; evaluating a human simulator requires evidence about the behaviour it is intended to represent. These objectives inform different questions about agent actions and outcomes.

### Questions

- Whose preferences do agent-mediated interaction logs represent?
- How are human needs and constraints retained during delegation?
- How do people review, correct, or take over agent decisions?
- How does mixed human and agent behaviour affect system learning and subsequent interaction?
